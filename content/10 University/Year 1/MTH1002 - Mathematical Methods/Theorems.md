# Theorems

### Cross Product Orthogonal Properties
Let $\vec{u}$ and $\vec{w}$ be vectors in $\mathbb{R}^3$. Then $\vec{u} \times \vec{w}$ is orthogonal to both $\vec{u}$ and $\vec{w}$.

### Cross Product
Let $\vec{u}$ and $\vec{w}$ be vectors in $\mathbb{R}^3$, and let $\theta$ be the angle between $\vec{u}$ and $\vec{w}$. Then $$|\vec{u} \times \vec{w}|^2 = |\vec{u}|^2 |\vec{w}|^2 - (\vec{u} \cdot \vec{w})^2 = |\vec{u}|^2 |\vec{w}|^2 \sin ^2 \theta$$

### Dot Product Orthogonal properties
Let $\vec{u}$ and $\vec{v}$ be non-zero vectors. Then $\vec{u} \perp \vec{v}$ if and only if $\vec{u} \cdot \vec{v} = 0.$

### Dot Product
If $\vec{u} = (u_1, u_2, u_3)$ and $\vec{v} = (v_1, v_2, v_3)$, then $$\vec{u} \cdot \vec{v} = u_1 v_1 + u_2 v_2 + u_3 v_3.$$
