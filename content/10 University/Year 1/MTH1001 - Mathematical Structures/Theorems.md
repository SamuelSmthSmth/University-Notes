### Conditions for inverse of a function
A function $f : A \to B$ has an inverse *iff* $f$ is a bijection. Further, let $f : A \to B$ and $g : B \to C$ be bijections. Then, $$g \circ f : A \to C$$ is also a bijection, and it's inverse is $$(g \circ f)^{-1} = f^{-1} \circ g^{-1}$$

### Lagrange's theorem
Let $G$ be a finite group, and let $H$ be a subgroup of $G$. Then $|H|$ is a factor of $|G|$.

### Parity of a permutation
A permutation cannot be expressed as both a product of an even number of transpositions and of an odd number of transpositions.
