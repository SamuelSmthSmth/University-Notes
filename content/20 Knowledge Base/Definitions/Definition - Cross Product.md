Let $\vec{u}, \vec{w} \in \mathbb{R}^3$. The *vector cross product* of $\vec{u}$ with $\vec{w}$ is given by $$\vec{u} \times \vec{w} = 
\begin{vmatrix}
\hat{i} & \hat{j} & \hat{k} \\
u_1 & u_2 & u_3 \\
w_1 & w_2 & w_3
\end{vmatrix}
= \left( u_2w_3 - u_3w_2, -u_1w_3 + u_3w_1, u_1w_2 - u_2w_1 \right).$$
