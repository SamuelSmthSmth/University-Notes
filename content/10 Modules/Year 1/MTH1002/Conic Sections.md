### Subspaces
We moved on to define a subspace. This is essentially a "vector space inside a vector space."

![[Def - Subspace]]

To prove a subset is a subspace, we use the **Subspace Test**:
1. Non-empty (contains $\mathbf{0}$)
2. Closed under addition
3. Closed under scalar multiplication
